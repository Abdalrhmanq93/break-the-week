A visual school week for young children

Every school day is a big, glossy block on the Weekend Road, with picture stickers for what happens that day: swimming, library, sports kit, a school trip. After school, your child taps today’s block with the Star Hammer until it smashes into sparkles. When the last block is gone, the weekend chest opens with fireworks, a trophy and the family’s weekend plans.

See the whole week

One block per school day shows how many are left. A new row drops from the sky every week.

Plan it with stickers

32 picture stickers for school days and weekends. Day stickers repeat like a timetable.

Zaki reads it aloud

A friendly robot coach speaks every message, with a big repeat button, for kids who can’t read yet.

English and العربية

Fully bilingual: the app, the voice and the weekday names follow your iPad’s language.

Made by a parent, for families

  • Why it exists. Break the Week was made for a six-year-old who asked “Is it the weekend yet?” every morning. It turns the school week into something a child can see and hold on to.
  • Any school week. Monday to Friday, Sunday to Thursday or a Friday-to-Sunday weekend: you choose the school days.
  • Grown-ups are in charge. A parental gate protects the Parent Zone: week planner, holidays, an after-school time, sound and voice.
  • Gentle by design. No timers and no fail states, and rewards are never taken away.
  • Private. No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts, no tracking. It works completely offline, and nothing leaves the iPad.

Requires an iPad with iPadOS 17 or later.
