A weekend countdown kids can play

Each school day is a big, colorful voxel block on the Weekend Road. After school, your child grabs the golden Star Pick and taps today’s block: it cracks, wobbles and shatters into a shower of cubes and gems. When the last block is gone, the weekend treasure chest bursts open with fireworks and a dance party.

See the week

One block per school day shows how many days are left. A new row drops from the sky every week.

Smash today’s block

Four taps, a big crash and flying gems. Everyone cheers.

Unlock the weekend

Fireworks, a treasure chest, Ember the baby dragon and a dance party.

No reading needed

Bolt the robot coach reads every message aloud, and there’s a big repeat button.

Made for families

  • Grown-ups are in charge. A parental gate protects the Parent Zone, where you choose school and weekend days, add holidays, set an after-school time, and control sound, music and voice.
  • Rewards that keep kids going. A trophy for every week, streaks, badges and a shop paid for with gems earned in the game.
  • Gentle by design. No timers and no fail states, and rewards are never taken away.
  • Private. No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts, no tracking. It works completely offline, and nothing leaves the iPad.

Requires an iPad with iPadOS 17 or later.
